# Governance and Intellectual Property Policy

> **Notice:** This policy describes how the proprietary technology of **PoWV Protocol** is protected and who holds the rights to it. The proprietary cyber-physical infrastructure, source code and hardware designs of this project are not in the public domain.

## 1. Scope of the Trade Secret

Technical information about PoWV Protocol that has not been made public, whether through publication or an open-source license, is treated as confidential. Where it meets the legal requirements, it is protected as a **trade secret** and is the exclusive intellectual property of the protocol's founder, held through the registered business identified at the end of this policy. Material that the project has published, including its public documentation, is not a trade secret, but remains subject to copyright.

This includes, but is not limited to:
* **Cyber-Physical Architecture:** The integration logic between physical sensors (telemetry, GNSS, computer vision), IoT components, and secure hardware elements.
* **Firmware and Edge Logic:** Signal processing algorithms (e.g., FFT), industrial scale calibration, and *Edge Computing* communication protocols.
* **Source Code and Smart Contracts:** Convergence, validation, and cryptographic chaining logic used to generate PoWV evidence.
* **Internal Documentation:** Architecture diagrams, bench hardware schematics, and structural *Tokenomics* not yet disclosed to the public.

## 2. Regulatory Framework and Jurisdiction (Brazil)

The protection of the PoWV Protocol's Intellectual Property (IP) is governed by Brazilian law, in particular the **Brazilian Industrial Property Law (Law No. 9,279/96)**, the **Software Law (Law No. 9,609/98)** and the **Copyright Law (Law No. 9,610/98)**.

Under **Article 195, items XI and XII, of Law No. 9,279/96**, the crime of unfair competition is committed by anyone who, without authorization:
* Discloses, exploits or uses confidential knowledge, information or data usable in industry, commerce or the provision of services, to which they had access through a contractual or employment relationship, even after that relationship has ended.
* Discloses, exploits or uses such knowledge or information obtained by unlawful means or through fraud.

## 3. Access Control and Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA)

The protection measures adopted by the PoWV Protocol are:
* **Private Repositories:** Access to closed code and architecture repositories on GitHub is restricted to individually authorized persons.
* **Physical Access (Test Bench):** No physical or legal person (including developers, partners, investors, or consultants) is authorized to inspect the hardware test bench or access development terminals without first signing a **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)**.
* **IP Assignment Clause:** Any third-party contribution to the project (code or hardware) is conditional upon the full and irrevocable assignment of the economic (patrimonial) Intellectual Property rights to the rights holder identified at the end of this policy.

## 4. Legal Remedies and International Protection

The PoWV Protocol does not tolerate the theft of its Intellectual Property (IP), unauthorized reverse engineering of its non-public technology, or the unauthorized disclosure of its confidential information.

### 4.1 Remedies in Brazil
In the event of a violation, the rights holder may pursue, as appropriate:
*   **Criminal Complaint:** A complaint to the competent authorities for unfair competition and violation of confidential information under Law No. 9,279/96.
*   **Civil Action:** A lawsuit for injunctive relief and compensation for losses and damages, including lost profits.
*   **Contractual Remedies:** Termination of contracts, partnerships or Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) with the infringing party, and enforcement of the contractual penalties they provide.

### 4.2 International Protection
Outside Brazil, the PoWV Protocol's Intellectual Property and trade secrets may also be protected:
*   **Applicable Treaties:** Under the **Berne Convention for the Protection of Literary and Artistic Works** (copyright, including software) and the **WTO TRIPS Agreement**, whose Article 39 covers undisclosed information.
*   **Foreign Law:** Violations abroad may be pursued under the domestic law of the relevant jurisdiction (e.g., the Defend Trade Secrets Act in the United States or Directive (EU) 2016/943 in the European Union).

### 4.3 Digital Custody and Preservation of Records
To support the reasonable protection measures expected for trade secrets, PoWV Protocol maintains the following practices:
*   **Access-Controlled Custody:** Proprietary source code, specifications and internal documentation are kept in private repositories of this GitHub Organization. Access is granted individually, limited to what each person needs, and reviewed by the rights holder identified at the end of this policy.
*   **Versioning:** Changes are recorded through version control. Each commit carries an author identity and a timestamp, and the hosting platform may add a signature. Where branch protection is enabled, protected history cannot be overwritten or deleted without administrative action.
*   **Records Available as Evidence:** Commit history, platform signatures, timestamps and, while the platform retains them, access and audit records may be offered as evidence of authorship, of the dates on which material existed and of the protection measures in place. Their weight is decided by the competent court or arbitral tribunal.
*   **Preservation:** When a dispute or investigation is reasonably anticipated, the rights holder will export and preserve the relevant records, compute and record cryptographic hashes of the exported files and, where appropriate, obtain independent certification, such as a notarial record (*ata notarial*) or a qualified timestamp.

These practices do not by themselves establish ownership, validity or infringement of any right.
